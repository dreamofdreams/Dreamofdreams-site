Transforming Purpose into Legacy

When people experience the result of my work, I want them to feel the overwhelming weight of God's love and the reality of the spiritual war being fought for their souls. I am ensuring my efforts are sustainable for the long term by rooting every word and action in daily prayer and constant communion with the Lord, understanding that this work is not mine, but His. My journey has shaped me to interact with others with more patience and a greater urgency to share the truth of the Gospel. The "next chapter" of this vision is to expand the message of Dream of Dreams to reach those who have lost their way, preparing them to hold onto their faith even when the Ruby of Faith is no longer in their possession. If I could leave only one lesson behind, it is this: Never underestimate the power of a heart completely surrendered to the Father, for through Him, even the smallest light can pierce the deepest dark.
