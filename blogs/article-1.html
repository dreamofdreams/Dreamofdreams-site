The Anatomy of a Dream

A fleeting wish is a desire that moves further away from one because one only wishes it. A purposeful dream is a vision you have that you work towards making it reality—which is one of your many purposes in life. I realized Dream of Dreams: The Prophecy Begins was one of my great purposes when I became drawn to complete it under any circumstances, even though I felt and knew Satan attempted to hinder and stop me. God gives us free will, but He is also there with you when you come to Him. As 2 Chronicles 15:2 says, "The Lord is with you while you are with him. If you seek him, he will be found by you." If I had never gone to the Lord, I wouldn't be writing this today. To pursue this, I had to confront self-doubt and the fear of failing, of being judged and condemned by the world. But 1 John 4:4 states, "Greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world." When you walk in the Holy Spirit, you overcome all these fears. My definition of success has not changed; my success has been the fear of the Lord. This is the source of my success throughout life. If you are struggling to find your dream, seek the Lord, ask Him to guide you to your purpose that glorifies Him, and don't give up.
