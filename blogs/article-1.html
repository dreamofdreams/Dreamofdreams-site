The Anatomy of a Dream

A fleeting wish moves farther away when it remains only a wish. A purposeful dream is a vision you choose to pursue. Dream of Dreams: The Prophecy Begins became one of my great purposes, even when fear, doubt, and opposition tried to stop me. God gives us free will, yet He remains with us when we turn to Him. As 2 Chronicles 15:2 says, ‘The Lord is with you while you are with him. If you seek him, he will be found by you.’ I would not be writing this today without learning to face self-doubt, failure, and judgment. 1 John 4:4 reminds us, ‘Greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.’ Walking in the Holy Spirit helped me overcome those fears. My definition of success has not changed: fear of the Lord is the foundation of my success. If you are struggling to find your purpose, seek the Lord, ask Him to guide you, and do not give up.
