1. How central is the romance? Think of it as a dial, not a switch, the quest usually leads, with the relationship shaping choices or driving alliances.

2. How does the romance pay off? Some relationships resolve within a single volume; others stretch across a series. Neither is better, but they ask different things of you.

3. How dark does it get? Adult epic fantasy often includes violence, grief, and loss. If you are in a hard season of life, choose books whose tone matches what you can carry right now. There is no shame in setting a book down and picking up a gentler one.

It also helps to label the relationship dynamic you enjoy, allies-to-lovers, rivals, childhood friends, and the pacing you prefer. And if the quest in your own life feels long right now, remember that the stories we love most are rarely the ones where everything goes smoothly.
